# Cap late fees at ten dollars

## Why

A copy returned months late owes more in fees than the library would pay to replace it, and a
member who owes that much stops coming back. A cap keeps the fee a nudge rather than a debt.

## What Changes

- A late return still owes 25 cents a day, but never more than 1000 cents (ten dollars) for
  one loan.

## Impact

- Artifacts: `service`
- Affected specs: `overdue`
